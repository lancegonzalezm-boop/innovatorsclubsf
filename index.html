Engineering, end to end

Innovators Club is a place where students discover ideas and builds, design their own projects, and develop proof-of-concept prototypes and various creations. We start from a drafting stage and end with an idea that flies, rolls, or lights up. We’ll teach CAD, coding, and mechanics along the way. You’ll embark on many projects involving modeling sci-fi or real applications, make flying drones, and create fun programs or scripts.

Bring your curiosity. We bring the knowledge.
