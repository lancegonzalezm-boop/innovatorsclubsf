> mission log
To become a successful engineer, you have to fail.

Every real engineer has a pile of old prototypes that never worked. That's not a problem with the process. That is the process. Innovators Club gives students a low-stakes place to break things, figure out why they broke, and build a better version next time. We care more about how much you improve than about getting it right the first time. If your first design works perfectly on the first try, you probably didn't push it hard enough.

The Cycle

How we actually build things

01
Prototype

Design it, build the first version, no matter how rough.

02
Test

Run it. Fly it. Load it. Find out what actually happens.

03
Fail

Something breaks, snaps, or doesn't fit. Expected, not a setback.

04
Diagnose

Figure out exactly why it failed, not just that it did.

05
Rebuild

Fix that one thing. Loop back to 01. Repeat until it holds.
